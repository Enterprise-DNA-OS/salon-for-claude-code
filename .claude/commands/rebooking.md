---
description: The rebooking rate, per chair - what share of the last 28 days' visits walked out holding their next appointment. The number a salon lives or dies on.
---

1. Run `node scripts/salon.mjs rebooking --json`.
2. Present the table per stylist and the whole-team line. Name the chair with the lowest rate without drama: the fix is a habit at the till, not a talking-to.
3. If asked why it matters: a client who leaves rebooked returns in their usual gap; one who does not drifts to whoever is nearest when the fringe annoys them. The lapsed list is where un-rebooked visits end up.
