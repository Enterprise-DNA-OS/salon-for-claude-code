description: The money - services and retail by chair for any window, average ticket, retail per visit - straight off the record, not a dashboard's version of it.
---

1. Run `node scripts/salon.mjs takings --json` (last 7 days) or `takings --days=28 --json` for the month.
2. Present per chair: visits, services, retail units, retail dollars. Then the totals: services + retail, average ticket, retail per visit.
3. Retail per visit is the number to watch beside rebooking: recommendations at the basin, not the counter, move it.
