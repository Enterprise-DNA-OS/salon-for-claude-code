---
description: Put a conversation on the client's record - the no-show call, the complaint made right, the "only books with Ava" - so the whole front desk knows what one person knows.
---

1. Run `node scripts/salon.mjs note add NAME "what happened"` the moment it is told to you, with the client's own words where they matter.
2. Reading back: `notes NAME`.
3. A note about a decision (a refused colour, an honoured expired voucher, a deposit now required) sits beside the action, not instead of it.
